About This Collection

Rooted in Tradition,
Refined for Today

From ornate idols and ceremonial vessels to modern home accents, our brassware collection represents centuries of metalworking tradition. Each piece is individually hand-cast, hand-hammered, or spun by master craftsmen who have inherited their skills across generations. We work with brass, bronze, copper, and mixed metals to produce artifacts that carry both aesthetic beauty and cultural depth.

Our metal artifacts span a wide range — from antique-finish decorative items and traditional dhokra work to contemporary brushed-metal pieces suited for modern interiors. All products undergo rigorous quality inspection and are available in custom finishes — oxidised, lacquered, polished, or antiqued — to meet diverse client specifications for retail, hospitality, and export.

Whether you require a single statement piece for a luxury residence or a bulk supply of coordinated artifacts for a hotel chain, our production facility and artisan network are equipped to deliver at scale without compromising on craftsmanship.

Traditional Hand-Casting

Each artifact is individually cast using time-honoured lost-wax and sand-casting techniques passed down through generations of craftsmen.

Multiple Metal Finishes

Available in antique, polished, oxidised, brushed, and lacquered finishes — customised to match your interior palette or brand standards.

Export-Ready Packaging

Every piece is individually wrapped and packed to international export standards, ensuring safe delivery for bulk and retail orders worldwide.
