About This Collection

Every Table Tells
a Story

Complete your tablescape with our curated range of handcrafted tabletop accessories. Featuring napkin rings, bread baskets, candlestick holders, serving trays, centrepieces, and coaster sets, each piece is designed to bring elegance and visual cohesion to your table setting. Our collection is a favourite among hospitality buyers, interior stylists, and home décor enthusiasts alike.

Each accessory is finished to complement both classic and contemporary table settings. We offer coordinated sets across different materials and finishes — polished brass, brushed steel, oxidised copper, and lacquered bronze — so your entire tablescape maintains a consistent and considered aesthetic language.

Our tabletop pieces are ideal for gifting as standalone items or as part of curated hampers. Custom sizing for specific centrepieces and serving trays is available on request, with minimum order quantities suited to both retail and bulk buyers.

Coordinated Collections

Available in matched sets across brass, steel, and mixed metal finishes, enabling a cohesive and intentional tablescape at any scale.

Hospitality-Ready

Trusted by luxury hotels, fine-dining restaurants, and event planners for premium table setups that make a lasting impression.

Custom Sizing

Bespoke dimensions available for specific centrepieces, serving trays, and display items to meet your exact spatial requirements.
