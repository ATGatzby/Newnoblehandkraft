About This Collection

Elevate Every
Pour

Elevate the art of hosting with our handcrafted barware and bar equipment collection. From copper Moscow Mule mugs and hammered cocktail shakers to ice buckets, bar trays, wine holders, and serving sets, every piece is designed to turn your bar into a showcase of craft and style. Perfect for the discerning home entertainer and hospitality professional alike.

Our barware range covers everything a well-appointed bar requires — cocktail tools, serving vessels, decanters, and display pieces. Materials include pure copper, hammered brass, and high-grade stainless steel, with finishes ranging from bright polished to antique and hand-hammered textured surfaces.

A standout choice for premium gifting, our barware sets are available in coordinated collections — making them ideal for wedding gifts, festive hampers, and executive corporate gifts. Custom engraving and personalised packaging available for all sets.

Pure Copper & Brass

Authentic copper and brass construction throughout the range, offering superior thermal properties, natural antimicrobial qualities, and lasting visual appeal.

Hand-Hammered Texture

Signature hand-hammered finishes available across the range — each texture is unique, giving every piece an artisanal character that machine production cannot replicate.

Hospitality Grade

Designed for high-frequency use in restaurants, bars, and hotels — durable, hygienic, and built to perform beautifully under demanding commercial conditions.
